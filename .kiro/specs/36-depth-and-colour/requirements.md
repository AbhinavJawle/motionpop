# Spec 36 requirements: depth and colour

Founder brief (2026-11-22): "the background is flat and boring … the hero background can be loud and nice and others can be subtle. just not boring. i want the editor in our hero showing like 20-30 percent … the pip mascots are scattered all over, position them meaningfully. make the pip on the button cta move a bit more when idle … 10-20% deviation from capy.ai … fix alignments and white space."

## R1 Hero
- R1.0 THE SYSTEM SHALL keep the hero's layout as approved in spec 34 (left poster headline, body left, Pip's button and "Watch it work" right). Only the background changes. (Founder follow-up: "dont change the hero layout … dont blatantly copy the reference … nice and subtle and not boring, nice if it's animated".)
- R1.1 THE SYSTEM SHALL draw the hero on lit storyboard paper (a faint grid plus slowly drifting Pip-blue, ember and violet light), not plain white.
- R1.2 THE SYSTEM SHALL show the voice as a wide coloured waveform that breathes behind the top edge of the editor.
- R1.3 WHEN the page loads at 1440 x 900 THE SYSTEM SHALL show 20–30 % of the real editor screenshot above the fold, rising from the horizon.
- R1.4 WHILE reduced motion is requested THE SYSTEM SHALL keep the hero still (no drifting light, no breathing waveform).

## R2 Sections
- R2.1 THE SYSTEM SHALL give the sections below the hero subtle colour and texture (pale washes, coloured halftone, ruled storyboard paper) so no long run of the page is plain white.
- R2.2 THE SYSTEM SHALL close the page on a scene that bookends the hero: the same waveform, with Pip's plane crossing a sunset.
- R2.3 THE SYSTEM SHALL fix the alignment and white space found in review (hero gap, the empty space above the product frame and under How, the staggered looks grid, the floating section actions).

## R3 Pip
- R3.1 Each Pip appears where its action means something: the clapper marks the take in How, the juggler juggles the fiddly bits, the stitched plush says "It's alive!", the plane flies the finished video out in the closer, and the sign-up Pip waits on the button.
- R3.2 WHILE idle THE SYSTEM SHALL make the sign-up Pip visibly wait: look left, look right, drum its paws on the button edge, blink.
- R3.3 WHEN the button is hovered or focused THE SYSTEM SHALL turn Pip back to face the visitor.

## R4 Originality
- R4.1 THE SYSTEM SHALL replace the round spot icons (a capy.ai device) with our own section markers drawn from the editor's vocabulary (timeline clips with timecodes), and show scroll progress as a playhead in the nav.

## R5 App screens
- R5.1 THE SYSTEM SHALL give the paper app screens (dashboard, start, styles, settings, processing, sign-in) a subtle coloured desk background instead of flat white, keeping frames white so they still read.
