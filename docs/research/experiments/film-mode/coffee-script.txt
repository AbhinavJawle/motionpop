Why does the whole world run on one little bean?

People drink about 2 billion cups of coffee every single day.

It started in Ethiopia. Traders carried it across the sea to Yemen, then on to Turkey, Italy and Brazil. Today Brazil grows more coffee than any other country.

Look at what happens to one cup. The farmer gets 10 cents, the roaster gets 50 cents, the café charges 4 dollars.

Home brewing versus the café. 1 dollar a cup at home, 5 dollars out. Same bean, five times the price.

Three reasons we can't quit. The caffeine, the ritual, and the excuse to meet someone.

In 1450 coffee reached Yemen. In 1650 the first coffeehouse opened in England. In 1971 the first big coffee chain opened in Seattle.

A barista in Rome once told me, "You don't drink coffee, you drink the morning."

This is the photo that sums it up: a long line of people outside a café at 7 am, all waiting for the same small cup.

One bean. 500 years. And it still wins every morning.
