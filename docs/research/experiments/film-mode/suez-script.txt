In March 2021, one ship stopped 12% of world trade. The Suez Canal is a narrow shortcut between the Mediterranean and the Red Sea. The Ever Given is 400 metres long. That's four football pitches, end to end. And it got wedged sideways. Every day it sat there, $9.6 billion of goods went nowhere. 369 ships queued up at both ends. Others gave up and sailed around Africa: 3,500 nautical miles further, and up to two weeks longer. It took six days to pull it free. One 400-metre mistake. One global lesson.
