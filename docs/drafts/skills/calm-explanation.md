## Intent
Explain clearly. Give the viewer time to understand. Nothing should feel rushed or showy.

## Shot sequence (timing as % of duration)
1. **0–15%**: Title or question appears cleanly.
2. **15–80%**: Sequential reveals with generous pauses. Each element gets space.
3. **80–100%**: Final frame holds with all elements visible for review.

## Camera
- Minimal or static
- Optional: very slow drift (scale 1.0 → 1.02 across entire duration)
- No sudden moves

## Emphasis
- Use hierarchy through size and position, not color
- Accent color sparingly, only for the most important element
- Consistent spacing and alignment

## Sound cues
- Minimal or none
- Optional: very subtle whoosh on transitions (low volume, ~0.3)
- No impacts or attention-grabbing sounds

## Rules
- Each element must be readable for at least 1.5 seconds
- Never overlap text reveals (one thing at a time)
- Generous padding and clear hierarchy
- Smooth, continuous easing (no bounce or elastic)

## Anti-patterns
- ❌ Fast cuts or rapid motion (breaks the calm)
- ❌ Overlapping reveals (confuses)
- ❌ Loud or dramatic sounds
- ❌ Accent color everywhere (dilutes meaning)

## Voice sync (if voiceover present)
- Each reveal syncs to the relevant phrase
- Pauses in voice should match visual holds
- The pacing follows speech, not a fixed rhythm
