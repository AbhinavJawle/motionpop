## Intent
A short, confident branded moment.

## Shot sequence (percent of duration)
- 0–30%: brand shape/colour wipe in
- 30–60%: logo or name resolves (mask reveal) on the spoken name
- 60–85%: tagline or CTA slides in
- 85–100%: quick hold, crisp exit

## Camera
Static or tiny scale settle 1.02 → 1.00.

## Emphasis
Brand colours from the brand kit.

## Sound cues
Short whoosh + pop.

## Rules
- Uses the user's own logo/brand only. ≤ 3 seconds.

## Anti-patterns
- Long intros, glitch overload, third-party logos.

## Voice sync
keyMoment = start of the spoken brand/product name.
