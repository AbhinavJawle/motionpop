## Intent
Make the difference between two things instantly obvious.

## Shot sequence (percent of duration)
- 0–20%: frame splits (vertical in 16:9, horizontal in 9:16); side A label enters
- 20–45%: side A value/visual builds
- 45–75%: side B builds and lands on the key word, visibly bigger/smaller
- 75–100%: difference callout (e.g. "3× more") in accent; hold

## Camera
Static frame, gentle push-in 1.00 → 1.03 after side B lands.

## Emphasis
Side B (the point being made) uses the accent; side A muted.

## Sound cues
Click on split, pop on side B landing.

## Rules
- Same scale for both sides (honest comparison). Labels ≤ 3 words each.

## Anti-patterns
- Different scales, decorative VS badges, both sides in accent colour.

## Voice sync
keyMoment = start of the word naming side B or the difference.
