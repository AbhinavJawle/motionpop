## Intent
Fast cuts, loud colour, big reactions.
Energy over explanation: every beat changes something on screen.

## Pacing
Scenes run 0.9–2.6 seconds.

## Layout mix
- speaker: 30%
- split: 30%
- full: 25%
- card: 15%

## Preferred templates
punch, counter, compare, stack, image, logos, statement

## Hook
Open on the moment: the clip, the score or the wildest claim, in one punchy line.
Default hook style: headline.

## Visual grammar
Short scenes, punchlines on speaker shots, rankings as stacks, versus as compare cards. Energy over explanation; every beat changes something on screen.

## Music
High-energy electronic, punchy drums, gritty bass, bright arps, 140 bpm.
Mood when set to auto: upbeat.

## Sound effects
Density: high.

## Banned
- real game logos or characters unless named
- controller clip art
- pixel-art filler
