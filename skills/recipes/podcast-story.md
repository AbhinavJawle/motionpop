## Intent
Room to breathe; the words carry it.
Fewer, longer scenes, with speaker shots for the emotional beats.

## Pacing
Scenes run 2.0–4.8 seconds.

## Layout mix
- speaker: 40%
- card: 25%
- split: 20%
- full: 15%

## Preferred templates
quote, image, statement, headline, punch

## Hook
Open mid-story: the most intriguing line, as a quiet title over the speaker.
Default hook style: cold-open.

## Visual grammar
Fewer, longer scenes. Quotes for the lines that matter, illustrations for places and moments described, speaker shots for the emotional beats.

## Music
Warm lo-fi, soft keys, brushed drums, gentle vinyl texture, 82 bpm.
Mood when set to auto: lofi.

## Sound effects
Density: low.

## Banned
- microphone clip art
- waveform filler
- busy lists
- shouting punchlines
