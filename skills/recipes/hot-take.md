## Intent
One bold claim, defended hard, built to be shared.
The reel is speaker-led and ends on the ask.

## Pacing
Scenes run 1.0–2.8 seconds.

## Layout mix
- speaker: 45%
- split: 30%
- full: 15%
- card: 10%

## Preferred templates
punch, statement, compare, headline, counter, stack

## Hook
Open on the claim itself, stated as bluntly as the speaker says it.
Default hook style: headline.

## Visual grammar
Speaker-led. Punchlines on the strongest words, statements with strike-throughs for what they reject, compare cards for wrong vs right. Ends on the ask.

## Music
Driving trap-influenced beat, deep 808, sharp claps, tense synth stab, 150 bpm half-time.
Mood when set to auto: dramatic.

## Sound effects
Density: high.

## Banned
- fire emojis
- clickbait arrows and circles
- fake shock faces
- invented statistics
