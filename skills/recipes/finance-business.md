## Intent
Numbers that land, charts that prove the point.
The tone stays calm and exact, with no hype.

## Pacing
Scenes run 1.5–3.6 seconds.

## Layout mix
- split: 35%
- card: 30%
- speaker: 25%
- full: 10%

## Preferred templates
counter, chart, compare, checklist, logo, statement, headline

## Hook
Lead with the number: the stat or the money amount the reel is about, counted up on its word.
Default hook style: stat.

## Visual grammar
Only numbers actually said become counters and charts. Before/after and this-vs-that become compare cards. Companies appear as logos. Calm, exact, no hype.

## Music
Measured cinematic pulse, muted piano, soft strings, steady low drum, 96 bpm.
Mood when set to auto: dramatic.

## Sound effects
Density: low.

## Banned
- stacks of cash
- rocket-to-the-moon imagery
- invented figures
- flashing red and green tickers
