## Intent
Make one number feel enormous and undeniable.

## Shot sequence (percent of duration)
- 0–15%: small context line enters (muted, eyebrow style)
- 15–55%: the number counts up with a decisive ease-out and lands exactly on the spoken number word
- 55–75%: accent colour/highlighter swipe on the final value; camera push settles
- 75–92%: label slides in under the number; hold
- 92–100%: fast exit (60% of entrance duration)

## Camera
Slow push-in 1.00 → 1.05 across the whole slot. No cuts.

## Emphasis
Final value in the accent colour; everything else muted. Number ≥ 22% of frame height (16:9), ≥ 30% (9:16).

## Sound cues
Soft "pop" on landing.

## Rules
- One number per frame. Label ≤ 6 words.
- Count from 0 unless the narration implies a starting value.

## Anti-patterns
- Bouncing digits, spinning numbers, more than one decorative element.

## Voice sync
keyMoment = start of the spoken number word.
