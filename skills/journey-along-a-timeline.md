## Intent
Carry the viewer through time with a sense of progress.

## Shot sequence (percent of duration)
- 0–15%: timeline line draws across the frame
- 15–80%: events appear in order as dots + labels, each landing on its spoken date; camera tracks along
- 80–100%: final event emphasised in accent; hold

## Camera
Horizontal track following the newest event (16:9); vertical track in 9:16.

## Emphasis
Current event bright, past events secondary, future events hidden.

## Sound cues
Soft tick per event.

## Rules
- Max 5 events visible; older ones fade to secondary.
- Dates exactly as spoken.

## Anti-patterns
- Showing all events at once; tiny unreadable labels.

## Voice sync
Each event's keyMoment = start of its spoken date/step word.
