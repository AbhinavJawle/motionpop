## Intent
Give the idea space to breathe; clarity over spectacle.

## Shot sequence (percent of duration)
- 0–20%: eyebrow label enters
- 20–60%: headline words reveal with gentle stagger
- 60–75%: highlighter swipe on the key phrase at the spoken key word
- 75–100%: subline fades in; long hold

## Camera
Very slow push-in 1.00 → 1.03.

## Emphasis
One highlighted phrase only.

## Sound cues
None or a very soft swell.

## Rules
- Headline ≤ 60 characters. Generous margins.

## Anti-patterns
- Fast cuts, multiple highlights, decorative shapes moving.

## Voice sync
keyMoment = start of the key phrase.
