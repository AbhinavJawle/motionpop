version: 1
use_when: the narration describes growth, decline or change over time

default_duration: 6
---
## Intent
Let the viewer feel momentum building, then land on where it ended up.

## Shot sequence (percent of duration)
- 0–15%: title and axes fade in, baseline draws
- 15–70%: data builds left to right (line draws on / bars grow in sequence), steady pace
- 70–80%: end point or highest bar emphasised at the key spoken word (dot pulse or accent fill)
- 80–100%: value label and optional annotation; hold

## Camera
Subtle drift following the data direction (x +1%), push-in 1.00 → 1.04.

## Emphasis
Only the final/most important data point gets the accent colour.

## Sound cues
Soft rising whoosh during the build, light tick on the emphasis.

## Rules
- Proportional geometry from zero baseline. Max 6 axis labels.
- The build must finish before the key word.

## Anti-patterns
- Animating all points at once; 3D charts; gridlines heavier than data.

## Voice sync
keyMoment = start of the word that names the end value or trend ("tripled", "forty percent").
