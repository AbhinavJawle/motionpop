## Intent
Build anticipation and pay off with the top item.

## Shot sequence (percent of duration)
- 0–10%: title ("The 3 biggest…") enters
- 10–75%: items appear from lowest to highest rank, each landing on its spoken name
- 75–90%: number one gets accent fill + slight scale (1.00 → 1.04)
- 90–100%: hold

## Camera
Static, push-in on the final item.

## Emphasis
Only #1 in accent.

## Sound cues
Tick per item, pop on #1.

## Rules
- Max 5 items. Horizontal bars in 9:16.

## Anti-patterns
- Revealing #1 first, podium graphics, confetti.

## Voice sync
Each item's keyMoment = start of its spoken name.
